Hands-on workshop

Teach a robot your dance

Motion retargeting and robot learning. A phone video of someone dancing becomes 3D human motion, gets retargeted to the Booster K1 humanoid, trained with reinforcement learning in simulation, and runs on the real robot.

How it works

From video to robot
→
→
  1. 01

    Motion capture

    Your video becomes a 3D body moving in space.

  2. 02

    Retargeting

    The human motion is mapped onto the robot's joints and proportions.

  3. 03

    Training in simulation

    Reinforcement learning teaches the robot to follow the motion without falling.

  4. 04

    Sim-to-real

    The trained policy runs on the real K1.

More dances on the real K1.

Tools: GVHMR (video to 3D motion), GMR (retargeting), MuJoCo and mjlab (simulation and training).

What to bring

  • A laptop.
  • A short video of yourself dancing is nice to have, but optional.
